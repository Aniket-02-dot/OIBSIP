# Exploratory Data Analysis on Retail Sales Data

## OIBSIP - Data Analytics Level 1 Task 1

### Project Overview

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on retail sales data to identify sales patterns, customer behaviour, product performance, and actionable business insights.

### Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze retail sales data using Python and identify meaningful patterns and trends that can support business decision-making.

### Tech Stack

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

### Analysis Performed

- Dataset shape, data types, and missing value analysis
- Descriptive statistics
- Monthly sales trend analysis
- Quarterly sales trend analysis
- Customer age-group analysis
- Gender distribution analysis
- Top 10 best-selling products
- Revenue by product category
- Quantity sold by product category
- Correlation analysis using a heatmap
- Additional visualization for customer age-group revenue
- Business insights and recommendations

### Key Insights

- Electronics generated the highest revenue among the original retail product categories.
- Clothing recorded the highest quantity sold among the original categories.
- The 46–55 age group had the highest number of customers.
- Female customers were slightly higher in number than male customers.
- Quarterly sales were highest in Q4 2023 in the original retail dataset.
- Sales showed noticeable fluctuations across different months.
- The Top 10 product analysis identifies products with the highest quantity sold in the product-level dataset.

### Business Recommendations

1. Maintain sufficient inventory for high-revenue product categories.
2. Use promotions and cross-selling strategies for high-volume categories.
3. Target the major customer age segments with personalized marketing campaigns.
4. Use historical sales trends to plan inventory and promotional activities.
5. Prioritize high-demand products to reduce stock-out risks.
